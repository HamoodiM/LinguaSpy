# Facilitator script (≈ 60 min + 10 min one week later)

**Before students arrive**
- [ ] Each laptop runs the build in pilot mode (`Laptop_Setup.md`). Headphones plugged in, volume checked.
- [ ] Consent and assent forms collected. Students without consent can still play but are **not** given a code and their laptop's log is deleted.
- [ ] A list of participant codes (MT-01, MT-02, …). Give each consenting student a code on a slip of paper. Do not write names next to the codes on anything that leaves the room.
- [ ] Observer sheets printed.

## 0–5 min: welcome
> "Thanks for helping test a language-learning game. You'll play a short spy mission in French. This isn't a test of
> you, it's a test of the game. If something is confusing, that's useful information for us. You can stop at any
> time. Before and after playing you'll answer about 15 quick questions. You won't see if each answer is right, so
> just do your best and guess if you're not sure."

Students enter their code on the first screen (Field tests → Participant code).

## 5–13 min: pre-test
They press **1. Before playing**. Don't help with answers. If someone finishes early, they wait quietly or read the
Settings screen.

## 13–48 min: play
- They press **Close** on the Field test panel, then click the **Paris** card.
- Let them play Operation First Contact, then Opération Croissant if there's time.
- **Don't coach.** If a student is stuck for more than 2 minutes, say only: "What is the handler telling you?" or
  "Try the Translate / Repeat button". Write the moment on the observer sheet.
- At 46 min: "Please finish the conversation you're in."

## 48–55 min: post-test
Press **P → Return to menu → Field tests → 2. After playing**.

## 55–60 min: survey and short chat
Hand out the survey (`Survey.md`). Optional: ask two or three students, "What was the best part? What was most
confusing?" and note the answers on the observer sheet.

## One week later (5–10 min)
Same laptops if possible. If not, see `Laptop_Setup.md` to move the save.
- Students may do a Safe house review first if they want. The log records whether they did.
- Then **3. One week later**.
