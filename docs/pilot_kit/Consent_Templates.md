## 2. Parent / guardian consent (participants under 18)
**What is this?** Mohammad Mohammad, a Computer Science student, built a game called LinguaSpy that teaches beginner
French. He would like to test whether it helps students learn and whether it is easy to use.

**What will my child do?** Answer about 15 short French questions, play the game for about 35 minutes, answer the
same kind of questions again, and fill in a short survey. One week later there are about 5–10 more minutes of
questions. All of it happens during a regular partner-organization session.

**What is recorded?**
- The game saves an anonymous code (like MT-07), the answers given in the game and the test, and timings.
- No name, photo, voice or location is recorded.
- The survey is labelled with the same code.

**Risks and benefits.** No known risks beyond normal screen use. Your child may learn some French phrases.

**Voluntary.** Your child can stop at any time without any consequence. You can ask for your child's data to be
deleted until ____________ (date of the summary report).

**Contact.** Mohammad Mohammad, [email].
