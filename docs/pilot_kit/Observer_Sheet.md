**Things to watch for**
- Do they read the briefing cards, or click through them?
- Do they find Translate, Repeat and Slower?
- Do they understand the hint after a first mistake?
- Can they type accents (é, à, ç)? Some keyboards make this hard.
- Where do they lose cover?
- Do they understand what to do after the debrief?
